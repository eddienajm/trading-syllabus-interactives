Each vertical line is one strike, read three ways: its price, its moneyness, its call delta. Switch between the spacings. An evenly spaced strike ladder is not evenly spaced in moneyness, and neither is evenly spaced in delta. Space evenly in delta and the wing strikes fly apart while the near-the-money strikes bunch together: delta is the moneyness axis pushed through the normal curve after a half-step shift, Δ = e−qTN(−x + σ√T/2), and the normal curve squashes its tails. The density is drawn against log price here, so it is a symmetric bell whose peak sits half a step left of the forward.
