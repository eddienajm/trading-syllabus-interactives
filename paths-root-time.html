Twelve simulated paths over three years. The shaded band is one standard deviation either side of the median, and it is a √t curve, not a wedge: it opens fast early and then flattens. The solid line is the mean and the dashed line is the median. Push volatility up and watch them separate while the drift is unchanged; the rest of this section explains why.
